# DialInLog — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Home baristas, café owners, coffee perfectionists. They've spent $1500+ on their machine. They care about grind size to the click, dose to 0.1g, yield to 0.5g, time to the second. Phone next to the espresso machine at 6:45am. Calibrating a new bag of beans. Yesterday's puck on the rim.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **precise, ritual, café-obsessive.**

This is for people who track shots like a chemist tracks experiments. Italian café aesthetic but engineering-grade discipline. Not a coffee marketing site. Not a Starbucks app.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Dark theme.** Espresso bar at 7am, brass fixtures, the morning ritual. Dark feels appropriate — coffee is a pre-dawn discipline.

**Font pair:**
- Display: `Big Shoulders Display` — heavy condensed display, signage-shop feel, like a vintage café awning or industrial espresso machine branding. NOT in reflex list.
- Body: `Encode Sans` — slightly geometric, technical sans, holds up at numerical-data sizes. NOT in reflex list.

**Color system (OKLCH, dark warm):**
- Background: `oklch(0.13 0.020 30)` — dark warm coffee-brown black
- Surface: `oklch(0.17 0.025 30)` — slightly lighter dark warm
- Foreground: `oklch(0.93 0.015 50)` — crema/warm cream
- Muted foreground: `oklch(0.62 0.015 50)`
- Border: `oklch(0.27 0.025 30)`
- Primary (copper/crema): `oklch(0.55 0.17 40)` — copper espresso shot
- Primary light: `oklch(0.68 0.15 40)`
- Accent (golden crema): `oklch(0.72 0.14 70)` — top of a perfect espresso shot

### Design Principles
1. **Ratios and times in display type.** "1:2 in 28s" should be displayed with engineering-grade weight.
2. **Tabular numbers everywhere.** font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on all data.
3. **No icon cards.** Use shot-log table aesthetics — dose / yield / time columns.
4. **Dark + copper feels like a real café**, not a tech-cyan dashboard.
5. **Brand mentions matter** — La Marzocco, Niche, Decent — these are tribe markers. Display them with respect.
